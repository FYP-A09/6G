Self-supervised learning on 5G-NIDD

After removing duplicate flows (66% of the 1.2 M) and identifier columns that leaked the label. Macro-F1 on 81,731 unseen flows, for 50 to all labelled flows.

Pretraining (masked-feature and contrastive) never beats the best raw-feature model at any label budget. Raw features alone reach 0.926 (0.936 with LightGBM); the old figure of 0.708 came from leaked and duplicated data.

When the traffic mix changes

Controllers trained or tuned on ordinary traffic and scored without retraining, held-out week.

The trained controllers lose to the hand-tuned rule once the mix changes. MARL's advantage is finding a good policy from data without hand tuning, and scaling to any number of cells.

Digital Twin dataset

Core year (400 cells x 364 days x 6 scenarios) and full city (10,000 cells x 62 recorded days x 3 scenarios), one row per cell per 10 minutes.

Earlier modules and checks
